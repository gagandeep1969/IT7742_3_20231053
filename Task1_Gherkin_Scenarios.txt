Feature: Intra-account money transfer

  As a bank app user
  I want to transfer money between my own accounts
  So that I can manage my money across account types

  Background:
    Given a customer has multiple bank accounts
    And the customer can be either a general customer or a staff customer

  Scenario: Successful transfer from Everyday account to Investment account
    Given the customer has an Everyday account with balance 500
    And the customer has an Investment account with balance 900
    When the customer transfers 200 from the Everyday account to the Investment account
    Then the transfer should be successful
    And the Everyday account balance should become 300
    And the Investment account balance should become 1100
    And no fee should be charged

  Scenario: Failed transfer from Everyday account due to insufficient balance
    Given the customer has an Everyday account with balance 500
    And the customer has an Investment account with balance 900
    When the customer transfers 600 from the Everyday account to the Investment account
    Then the transfer should fail
    And the Everyday account balance should remain 500
    And the Investment account balance should remain 900
    And no fee should be charged

  Scenario: Successful transfer from Investment account to Everyday account
    Given the customer has an Investment account with balance 1200
    And the customer has an Everyday account with balance 300
    When the customer transfers 200 from the Investment account to the Everyday account
    Then the transfer should be successful
    And the Investment account balance should become 1000
    And the Everyday account balance should become 500
    And no fee should be charged

  Scenario: Failed transfer from Investment account for general customer
    Given the customer is not a staff member
    And the customer has an Investment account with balance 900
    And the customer has an Omni account with balance 1100
    When the customer transfers 1000 from the Investment account to the Omni account
    Then the transfer should fail
    And a failed transfer fee of 15 should be charged to the Investment account
    And the Investment account balance should become 885
    And the Omni account balance should remain 1100

  Scenario: Failed transfer from Investment account for staff customer
    Given the customer is a staff member
    And the customer has an Investment account with balance 900
    And the customer has an Omni account with balance 1100
    When the customer transfers 1000 from the Investment account to the Omni account
    Then the transfer should fail
    And a failed transfer fee of 7.50 should be charged to the Investment account
    And the Investment account balance should become 892.50
    And the Omni account balance should remain 1100

  Scenario: Successful transfer from Omni account using available balance
    Given the customer has an Omni account with balance 1100
    And the Omni account has overdraft limit 150
    And the customer has an Everyday account with balance 400
    When the customer transfers 1000 from the Omni account to the Everyday account
    Then the transfer should be successful
    And the Omni account balance should become 100
    And the Everyday account balance should become 1400
    And no fee should be charged

  Scenario: Successful transfer from Omni account using overdraft
    Given the customer has an Omni account with balance 1100
    And the Omni account has overdraft limit 150
    And the customer has an Everyday account with balance 400
    When the customer transfers 1200 from the Omni account to the Everyday account
    Then the transfer should be successful
    And the Omni account balance should become -100
    And the Everyday account balance should become 1600
    And no fee should be charged

  Scenario: Failed transfer from Omni account for general customer beyond overdraft limit
    Given the customer is not a staff member
    And the customer has an Omni account with balance 1100
    And the Omni account has overdraft limit 150
    And the customer has an Everyday account with balance 400
    When the customer transfers 1300 from the Omni account to the Everyday account
    Then the transfer should fail
    And a failed transfer fee of 10 should be charged to the Omni account
    And the Omni account balance should become 1090
    And the Everyday account balance should remain 400

  Scenario: Failed transfer from Omni account for staff customer beyond overdraft limit
    Given the customer is a staff member
    And the customer has an Omni account with balance 1100
    And the Omni account has overdraft limit 150
    And the customer has an Everyday account with balance 400
    When the customer transfers 1300 from the Omni account to the Everyday account
    Then the transfer should fail
    And a failed transfer fee of 5 should be charged to the Omni account
    And the Omni account balance should become 1095
    And the Everyday account balance should remain 400

  Scenario: Transfer is not allowed to the same account
    Given the customer has an Everyday account with balance 500
    When the customer tries to transfer 100 from the Everyday account to the same Everyday account
    Then the transfer should fail
    And the account balance should remain 500
    And no fee should be charged
